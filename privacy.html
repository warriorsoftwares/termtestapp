Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Master's Quizzes ("the app") is a free educational quiz website made for Sri Lankan students and students around the world. This policy is provided by the main developer of Master's Quizzes. It explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: email address, password (stored securely by our login provider), username, age, grade, country, and optional school name.
  • Profile photo (optional, future feature): if you upload a profile photo when that feature is available, we will store the image to show on your account.
  • Quiz data: scores, progress, and related study or game stats.
  • Technical data: basic device or browser information needed for the website and installable app to work.

2. How we use this information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To run quizzes and save your results
  • To show your progress, high scores, and profile in the app
  • To improve the app and fix problems

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Services we use

  • Supabase — account login and storage of scores and related data
  • GitHub Pages — hosting the website

Those services process data under their own privacy policies as well.

4. Advertising

The app does not show ads at the moment. If we add advertising later (for example Google AdSense), third parties may use cookies or similar technology to show ads. We will update this policy if that happens. You can read more about Google’s advertising practices here: Google Advertising.

5. Children

Master's Quizzes is aimed at school students. Young children should use the app with a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian may contact us to ask about, or request deletion of, a child’s data.

6. Your choices

  • You may stop using the app at any time
  • You may request deletion of your account data by emailing us
  • School name is optional and does not have to be provided
  • Profile photo will be optional when that feature is added

7. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the main developer at:

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do.
